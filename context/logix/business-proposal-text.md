## Page 2 — The problem

**Data exists. *Intelligence doesn't.***

Fleet companies already have the data they need. Each system does its own job well, but **none of them talk to each other.**

| # | Heading | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Scattered across systems | Five or more GPS platforms, compliance, toll and expense tools, each holding one part of the picture. |
| 02 | Data without insight | Reports show numbers, not what needs attention. The data is there, but nothing explains it. |
| 03 | Not built around people | Information sits inside individual systems instead of one simple view anyone can open and act on. |
| 04 | Manual interpretation | Teams switch platforms and compare numbers by hand, spending hours before a decision. |

**A day in *operations*** — Someone needs a truck's location, fuel or an alert. First they work out which platform to open, then they dig for the answer.

> One question about one truck becomes **a search across several systems.**

## Page 4 — Proposed solution

**One platform. *Built around your systems.***

Logix sits on top of the systems you already run. It connects to **most fleet dashboards in use today** and turns their data into one clear picture: what is happening, why, and where to act.

| Card | Text |
|---|---|
| Cross-system intelligence | Insights that only appear when GPS, compliance, toll and expense data are read together. |
| A single view | Every vehicle, driver and trip in one place, whichever platform the data came from. |
| Actionable operations | Exceptions, alerts and reports go to the right person, so action is faster. |

**From data *to decisions***

1. **Connect** — Logix plugs into the dashboards and tools you already use.
2. **Understand** — Data from every source is brought into one structure and compared.
3. **Act** — AI finds patterns and exceptions and flags what needs attention.

**Nothing to replace. Nothing to retrain.** Your teams keep the tools they know. Logix brings them together.

## Page 5 — Proposed solution: where does AI close the gaps?

**Where does *AI close the gaps?***

We use AI where it does something faster or better: reading large volumes of vehicle, trip and driver data to **find anomalies a person would take hours to spot.**

| # | Area | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Trip and movement analysis | Planned versus actual routes, distance, stops and travel time for every trip. |
| 02 | Performance overview | Each vehicle scored on utilisation, idle time, loading and unloading. |
| 03 | Vehicle health and compliance | Challans, PUC, documents and service history, so nothing lapses. |
| 04 | Driver behaviour | Harsh driving, neutral coasting, excessive halts and long idle periods. |
| 05 | Fuel and expense variance | Fuel allowance against actual use, and payment mismatches. |
| 06 | Insights and smart feedback | Patterns turned into plain-language summaries, alerts and reports. |

> **Less searching. Less comparing. *Less guessing.*** The important signals come to you, so your team spends its time acting, not looking.
