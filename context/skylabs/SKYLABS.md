# Skylabs — Company Context (public-safe excerpt)

## Identity

**Name:** Skylabs
**Type:** Product-focused technology company
**Direction:** Building AI-powered products and systems around real-world business problems and specialised workflows.

Working positioning:
> Skylabs builds AI-powered products and systems for real-world business workflows.

Underlying belief:
> The best technology starts with understanding the work.

Skylabs should not be positioned as a generic design agency, generic AI agency, or a company that merely adds chatbots to existing software.

## What Skylabs wants to be known for

- Understanding real business workflows.
- Building purpose-oriented products, not demos for their own sake.
- Applying AI where it creates practical value.
- Combining product thinking with engineering execution.
- Working on complex, specialised industry problems.
- Being thoughtful and specific rather than loud or hype-driven.
